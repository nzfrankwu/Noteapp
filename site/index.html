For Android tablets with a stylus

Handwritten notes and your assignments, in one place.

Write with your pen in notebooks for each course, mark up lecture slides, and always know what's due next. Noteapp can read your assignments from Canvas and back everything up to your own Google Drive.

Download the APK View on GitHub

Free and open source · Android 8.0 or newer · No ads, no tracking

Write like on paper

Pressure-sensitive pens and highlighters, a whole-stroke or partial eraser, and press hard to erase. Pages sit side by side; pinch to zoom in on the details.

  • Hold the pen still to snap a straight line, rectangle, circle or triangle
  • Lasso to move, resize, turn or copy, then drag a shape's corners
  • Lined, grid, dot or blank paper, per notebook or per page

Mark up your lecture slides

Import a PDF, Word or PowerPoint file and write straight on it. Highlight what matters, circle what's on the exam and add your own notes in the margin.

  • Select PDF text to copy, highlight or underline
  • Export your annotated notes as a PDF
  • Add pictures, text boxes and screenshots

Know what's due

Every course gets a folder with its notebooks and tasks together. Noteapp reads your assignments from Canvas (read-only), so due dates turn up on their own, with reminders before they're due.

  • Track each task: started, stuck, double-checking, done
  • See the weeks ahead on a calendar or timeline
  • Link a task to the notebook pages you need for it

Find anything, even your handwriting

One search covers notebook names, tasks, PDF text, text boxes and your handwriting. Tap a result and Noteapp opens the page right where the words are, and points them out.

  • Handwriting is read on your tablet; nothing is uploaded
  • Forgiving with messy writing
  • Find in this notebook, from the editor

And the rest

Backed up to your DriveAutomatic backups to your own Google Drive, plus readable PDF copies of your notes.
Two notebooks at onceSplit view: your slides on one side, your notes on the other.
Light and darkFollows your tablet, or pick one. Course colours are yours to choose.
Nothing lost by accidentDeleted notebooks wait in Recently deleted for 30 days. Export everything to one file anytime.
Your notes stay yours. Noteapp has no servers and no accounts of its own, shows no ads and doesn't track you. Your notes live on your tablet and, if you connect it, in your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.

Install

  1. 1
    DownloadOn your tablet, open the latest release and download the .apk file.
  2. 2
    Open itAllow your browser to install apps when Android asks, then tap Install.
  3. 3
    UpdatesInstall a newer APK over the old one. Your notes stay, because every release is signed with the same key.

Made for tablets with a stylus (tested on an OPPO Pad 3 with OPPO Pencil 2), using only standard Android stylus features.
